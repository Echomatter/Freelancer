# Bounded judgment

Adapted from TypeSafe's official [agent skill](https://github.com/typesafe-ai/skills/tree/main/skills/typesafe-ai) for Freelancer's shared Jev MCP tools. Consider JEV when a cheap, closed judgment over collected evidence would help: classification, ranking, score, confidence or comparison among explicit alternatives. Supply relevant evidence and criteria. For a difficult comparison, a few bounded questions may help more than one broad question; skip obvious choices. Treat results as advisory and verify material claims independently. It is not autonomous decision authority, an orchestration gate, a substitute for research/testing or open-ended reasoning. If billing, authentication or availability fails, continue normal judgment without JEV.

Existing routing, delegation and review guidance offers contextual examples.
JEV can compare otherwise eligible choices or triage grounded findings; it cannot
determine eligibility, grant delegation, override a user's explicit model choice,
authorize paid use or change native permissions. Structured Freelancer outcomes
take precedence when sufficient actual evidence already settles model fit.

This skill is optional guidance. It does not grant or gate access to JEV or other tools.
